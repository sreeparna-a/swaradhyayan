The Portal

Music as a
living tradition.

Indian classical music is an oral tradition — passed from guru to shishya across centuries. Swaradhyayan preserves that depth while giving it a precise, navigable structure. Every raaga entry is annotated with aroh–avroh, pakad, chalan, characteristic phrases, swara distribution, and guru notes.

Beyond knowledge, the portal tracks your practice, surfaces assignments from your teachers, and recommends raagas that fit where you are in your journey.
